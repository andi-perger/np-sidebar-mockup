Help name the new platform’s navigation

We’re building the new BrightLocal platform, and the words in the navigation matter more than they might seem — they’re how every customer finds their way around. A few feature names are still up in the air, and before we lock them in we’d really value your take. To be clear, we’re not renaming the platform itself — just a handful of features within it — and there are no wrong answers. Pick what feels clearest to you, and tell us why.

What we’re trying to achieve

  • Clearer communication — names that mean the same thing to customers, support, sales and product, so we’re all talking about features the same way.
  • Names that work together — labels that make sense as a set in the navigation, not just on their own.
  • Less confusion, more clarity — especially where names currently overlap, like “AI Insights” and “Local AI Visibility”.

What we’re deciding

  1. What to call “AI Insights” — the feature that turns your local search data into a prioritised list of recommendations and actions on what to do next.
  2. How to name the visibility features — Local Search Grid, Local Rank Tracker and Local AI Visibility: branded product names, or plainer “what you get” language.
  3. Reputation Manager or Review Manager — this feature includes review insights, review management, review generation and review widgets. Do we use a branded product name like Reputation Manager, or something simpler like Reputation & Reviews?

As you look at each option, it helps to ask yourself: does the name say what the feature does? Would a brand-new customer understand it? And does it read well alongside the others?
